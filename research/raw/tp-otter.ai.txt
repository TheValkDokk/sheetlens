## [My experience with Otter.ai customer…](https://www.trustpilot.com/reviews/6abc041a176fd92cdd0ddddc)

My experience with Otter.ai customer support has been extremely frustrating and disappointing.

The biggest issue was not simply that I encountered a problem, but that the support process seemed incapable of actually understanding the issue and working toward a meaningful resolution. I had to spend far too much time explaining the situation, and even after providing details, the responses felt generic and disconnected from the actual problem I was trying to solve.

What should have been a straightforward support request turned into a long, drawn-out process. The amount of time it took to address the situation was completely unreasonable. Instead of feeling like I was working with a support team that was trying to diagnose and resolve the problem, it felt like I was repeatedly being redirected through standard responses without anyone taking ownership of the issue.

The so-called self-help resources were particularly disappointing. They may be useful for very basic questions, but when you have a specific technical or account-related problem, they don't provide the practical assistance needed to actually resolve it. Being sent to help articles that don't address the specific situation is not a substitute for effective customer support.

Another major frustration was the lack of clear communication and progress. When a customer is already dealing with a problem, having to repeatedly explain the same situation or wait extended periods for responses only makes the experience worse. At some point, the support process itself becomes more frustrating than the original problem.

I understand that not every issue can be resolved immediately, and I don't expect customer service to have an instant answer to everything. What I do expect is for the support team to understand the problem, investigate it properly, communicate clearly, and provide a reasonable path toward resolution. Unfortunately, that was not my experience with Otter.ai.

Overall, the combination of slow response times, lack of understanding, ineffective self-help resources, and what felt like a lack of ownership made this a very poor customer support experience. Otter.ai may have a useful product, but when something goes wrong, the support experience needs significant improvement.

If customer support is an important consideration for you, I would definitely keep this experience in mind before committing to the service.
