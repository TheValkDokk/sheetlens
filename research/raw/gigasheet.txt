## Frequently Asked Questions

How is Gigasheet priced?
Enterprise pricing is based on the data coverage, products, delivery methods, and licensing requirements your organization needs. We offer tailored annual subscriptions for interactive analytics, data licensing, and API access.

Can I purchase data for a specific specialty or state?
Yes. Gigasheet offers targeted data coverage for specific specialties, states, markets, payers, and providers, as well as broader regional and nationwide datasets.

Does pricing include the underlying data?
Gigasheet offers different licensing options depending on how you plan to use the data. Price Transparency Explorer combines healthcare data with interactive analytics, while bulk data and API offerings support integration into your own systems. Our team can help identify the right package.

Can I combine multiple Gigasheet products?
Yes. Organizations can combine negotiated rates, provider intelligence, analytics, APIs, and bulk data licensing based on their needs.

How current is Gigasheet's healthcare data?
Gigasheet continuously processes and refreshes published healthcare transparency data. Customers can also request processing of specific machine-readable files when their research requires data outside the standard processed inventory.

Can I evaluate the data before purchasing?
Yes. Our team can help you evaluate relevant market coverage and review representative data, product capabilities, or a tailored demonstration before making a purchasing decision.

Do you offer monthly subscriptions?
Enterprise healthcare data subscriptions are generally sold on an annual basis. Separate self-service subscriptions are available for individual data analysis needs.
