Specific problems, all new:

- The generation ignores explicit prompts. I asked it to remove an element and it returned the identical shot. 3 times. I asked for one visual style (the preset I'd committed to) and it rendered a completely different art style instead.

None of this happened on the version I used a few weeks earlier, with the same kind of inputs. A working pipeline became unreliable after a backend change.
