Who is Castmagic for?
Castmagic is for podcast hosts, video streamers, or long form content creators that want to get more mileage out of their content. We make it easy to instantly turn your long form unstructured content into ready-to-use copy for shownotes, highlight clips, summaries, blogs, social posts, newsletters, and more.

How does it work?
Castmagic uses the best in class AI to turn your long form audio directly into the exact content types you need for ready to post creative.

How does pricing work?
Every plan includes a growing library of transcribed hours — 30 on Hobby, 100 on Starter, and 400 on Team — that you build up over time, plus unlimited AI content, Studio clips, and integrations. Solo creators usually start on Hobby, Starter is the content home for a growing creator, and teams that need more seats, spaces, and API access move up to Team. Need more than that? Business & Scale add custom volume, more seats and storage, and dedicated support for studios and platforms. You can buy more hours anytime and upgrade or downgrade whenever you like.

What languages are supported?
Castmagic supports 60+ languages including English, Dutch, French, German, Hindi, Indonesian, Italian, Japanese, Korean, Mandarin (Simplified), Norwegian, Polish, Portuguese, Russian, Spanish, Swedish, Turkish, Ukrainian, and many more.
