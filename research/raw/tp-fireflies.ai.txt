## [Support is TERRIBLE](https://www.trustpilot.com/reviews/6ac66b228f92b6cd6e144192)

The Good:  
Web app is pretty good, particularly at discerning individual voices in a noisy environment.

The Bad:  
Mac desktop app is dreadful. Doesn't work properly, no audio recording available and whatever I do to turn the auto join setting off it tries to join every call I make on my Mac in google Meet, Zoom and Whatsapp. I deleted it in the end.

However, the worst aspect of Fireflies is the absolutely terrible support. I had a billing issue which has caused my account to be downgraded to the free version, losing access to all of my meetings. I cannot speak to anybody, and I'm just helplessly waiting for somebody to pick up my increasingly frantic emails and get back to me to restore my access. Meanwhile, I'm having to postpone calls until this access is restored, which fundamentally impacts my business.

Fireflies, like many other applications these days, hides behind a fairly useless AI support bot. I'd avoid it unless you have a robust backup in place. I'm certainly not going to rely on it going forward.
