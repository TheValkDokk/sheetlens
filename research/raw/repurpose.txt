## Questions? We've got answers.

What do I get with the Free Trial?

With the Free Trial, you'll have 14 days to use Repurpose. Within those 14 days, you can publish a total of 10 audios or videos to your favorite social media platforms absolutely free.

What happens when my Free Trial ends?

After the 14 days are over (or 10 videos published whichever comes first), you'll be asked to upgrade to one of our paid plans to continue using our platform. Simply add a credit card then choose a plan that meets your publishing needs.

How do I purchase a Repurpose subscription?

At any point during your Free Trial, you can choose to upgrade to one of our paid plans. Simply add a credit card then choose a plan inside the My Account page.
