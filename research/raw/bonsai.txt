## Frequently asked questions

How does the free trial work?

![Image 192: chevron down icon](https://cdn.prod.website-files.com/67db1ca556342937e18be8b9/67db1ca556342937e18be93e_interface-icon-chevron-down.svg)

When you sign up, your trial begins and you get full, unlimited access to Bonsai for 7 days. After the 7 days, you will be prompted to choose a plan and enter your credit card details if you haven't already to continue using Bonsai.

[Get started Get started](https://app.hellobonsai.com/users/sign_up)

Can I change my plan anytime?

![Image 193: chevron down icon](https://cdn.prod.website-files.com/67db1ca556342937e18be8b9/67db1ca556342937e18be93e_interface-icon-chevron-down.svg)

Yes, you can upgrade, downgrade or cancel your plan at any time via your Subscription settings.

Is my Bonsai subscription tax deductible?

![Image 194: chevron down icon](https://cdn.prod.website-files.com/67db1ca556342937e18be8b9/67db1ca556342937e18be93e_interface-icon-chevron-down.svg)

Yes, the IRS allows for tax deduction for software you use to run your business. Bonsai's expense tracking even automatically categorizes your subscription as a deductible software expense.

What are the payment fees

![Image 195: chevron down icon](https://cdn.prod.website-files.com/67db1ca556342937e18be8b9/67db1ca556342937e18be93e_interface-icon-chevron-down.svg)

Bonsai charges processing fees for online payments, which vary depending on the payment method used (e.g., credit card, ACH). These fees cover the cost of handling and transferring funds. For a full breakdown of payment methods and associated fees, please refer to our [help article](https://help.hellobonsai.com/en/articles/452273-understanding-online-payment-methods-and-fees-at-bonsai).
