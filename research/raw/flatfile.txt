## Frequently asked questions

Have a question we haven't addressed? Find answers to some of our most frequently asked questions below.

* * *

Flatfile does not store or see your credit card information or payment details. All payments are handled by [Stripe](https://stripe.com/), which is PCI Service Provider Level 1  certified and one of the largest, highly secure and most respected payment providers in the world.

* * *

All usage and billing is based on UTC (Universal Time Coordinated). As a result, depending on the time zone you are in, your account will be charged on the last day of the month or the first day of the following month.

* * *

Data usage for development/test purposes that happens in your development environment is always free. We of course expect fair use and that development environments are solely used for development and testing purposes.
